About

Biography

Md Sajid Hossain is a Senior Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, at American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB).

He has more than ten years of teaching experience and aims to bridge theoretical knowledge and practical application, for the benefit of his students and the institution. He has published research articles in several internationally recognised journals.

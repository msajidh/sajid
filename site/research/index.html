Research areas

Power quality

Custom power devices for distribution networks, such as the performance of a Dynamic Voltage Restorer (DVR) with a PID controller in mitigating voltage sags.

Renewable energy

Integrating wind power into the electrical grid, grid-stability challenges and wind resource assessment.

Machine learning

Applied deep learning, for example convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for automated plant disease detection.

Control systems

Classical controller and compensator design by root locus and frequency response.

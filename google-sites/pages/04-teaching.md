### Control Systems: compensator design in MATLAB
Worked MATLAB examples for PI, PD, PID, lag, lead and lag-lead compensator
design by root locus (Examples 9.1–9.6), plus a damping-ratio (ζ)
calculation script and a PDF on the design process.

- Browse the code: https://github.com/msajidh/sajid/tree/main/projects/matlab-control-compensators
- Design-process notes (PDF): https://github.com/msajidh/sajid/blob/main/projects/matlab-control-compensators/Design%20Process%20of%20PI%2C%20Lag%2C%20PD%20%26%20Lead%20Compensator.pdf

> Tip: upload the PDF to Google Drive and use **Insert → Drive** to show it
> inline for students.
